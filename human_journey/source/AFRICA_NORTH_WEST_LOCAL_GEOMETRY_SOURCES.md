# Sources and provenance

- Baseline pinned GitHub tree: https://github.com/petryshamen/minecraft-education/tree/82a8ee938eebba782da5a60c5b3159ccbb291f38/human_journey/source/data/human_journey/npc_geography . Exact Git blob and SHA256 manifests supplied for geography and water.
- Natural Earth 1:10m physical land, inland lakes, geographic label areas and river centreline vectors: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/ ; public domain https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ . Physical label polygons specifically approximate: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/10m-physical-labels/ . Frozen input source hashes provided; already acquired in Japan/Europe research.
- RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017, Dinerstein et al., 2017 https://doi.org/10.1093/biosci/bix014 ; CC BY 4.0 as documented in https://github.com/openlayers/data/blob/8ef2522c0b5a7a69fb59c44399ca7a9fbb7c1e8d/readme.md . Simplified mirror source https://github.com/openlayers/data/blob/8ef2522c0b5a7a69fb59c44399ca7a9fbb7c1e8d/docs/vector/ecoregions.json . Adapted selected source records frozen here. Coordinates are generalized to approximately 0.1° upstream, not high-resolution surveyed boundaries.
- GMBA Mountain Inventory v2: https://doi.org/10.48601/earthenv-t9k2-1407 , https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01256-y , CC BY 4.0, source mirror https://github.com/digital-landscape/AlpTap/tree/a0c05f948fdfcd5abe00bff510bb148fe7ae7614/public/data/mode-37dabf082c4a/regions . Five broad per-range source files retained. Original broad mountain regions do not define exact subrange labels.
- GSHHG 2.3.7: https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/wessel/gshhg/ ; https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/mgg/shorelines/shorelines.html . Low L2 water and high L3 lake islands from pinned mirror https://github.com/pmarguinaud/glgrib/tree/0dc6400ae53f692c4840f67e301dbb7a3eea2ba0/share/glgrib/coastlines/shp . LGPL/GPL notices retained. L2 large river records excluded; installed named water geometry overrides overlapping older lake sources. Frozen dry/land/water masks included.
- Installed HJ Water Features source at pinned production commit, including prior Africa Water Features. Read-only supporting reference; no Water Feature change. Prior source licence attribution remains intact.

Adaptations: clipping to research extent, projection, union/difference, topology simplification, matching existing Local concepts, dry-coverage restoration; no new country or political limits. Build scripts refer to already acquired shared NE/GSHHG source datasets in earlier workspace; selected adapted masks, exact candidate, audits, GMBA and ecological source subsets are self-contained for review and native testing. No actual Minecraft terrain image available.
